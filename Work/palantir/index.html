  • Contribute to highly reliable software used in defense and aerospace mission planning and decision support.

Knowledge, Skills and Abilities

  • Advanced software engineering in C++, Python, and Rust
  • Experience building distributed systems for operational environments
  • Knowledge of geospatial analytics, sensor fusion, and mission planning workflows
  • Strong collaboration and customer-facing engineering skills

Goals, Success Factors

  • Deliver reliable software for high-stakes defense and aerospace missions
  • Successfully transition capabilities from development to operational testing
  • Support customers with robust field-ready solutions
