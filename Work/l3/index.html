Algorithm Developer

2020-2025

L3Harris WESCAM

Job Description

  • Spearheaded research, implementation, and evaluation of cutting-edge algorithms for camera-based embedded computer vision applications, encompassing object detection, target tracking, sensor fusion, and state estimation.
  • Leveraged machine learning models to enhance tracking precision during irregular camera operation, thus mitigating potential tracking errors and enhancing reliability.
  • Rigorously tested UI to meet customer-specified interface updates.
  • Collected and meticulously analyzed extensive test data to validate software functionality and hardware reliability in real-world flight tests.
  • Ensured all systems met stringent performance criteria, delivering exceptional results in critical flight scenarios.
  • Facilitated a productive liaison for a collaborative computer vision research initiative with McMaster University, focusing on small target detection and tracking.
  • Collaborated with WESCAM’s Independent Stabilized Sighting Systems (MX-GCS) team to innovate new features for forthcoming electro-optic and infrared vision system offerings.
  • Pioneered the development of a radiometrically realistic synthetic data generator, significantly reducing the time required to gather field imagery and creating pseudo-realistic scenes.
  • Engineered a cutting-edge core target detection algorithm, utilizing computer vision, machine learning, and pixel-wise image processing techniques.

Reflection

Working at WESCAM was both exciting and challenging. The pandemic played a huge role in my onboarding and troubleshooting process. Given the escalating health concerns, most of my work was done from home. This provided a completely new work environment for me and my colleagues to adjust to. The work itself was also sufficiently challenging and pushed me to develop a deeper understanding of concepts I had learned throughout my master's. Working in the advanced processing group, our focus was primarily on research and development. This posed a significant challenge, as a lot of the data associated with the project was protected under the Controlled Goods Program (CGP). We were tasked with building robust algorithms while working with very limited requirements. Furthermore, it was interesting to see how security practices for development differed between WESCAM and my last job at General Dynamics.
