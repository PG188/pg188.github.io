McMaster Hyperloop

2015-2017

McMaster University

Project Description

The Hyperloop competition organized by SpaceX with the aim of helping accelerate the development of the Hyperloop prototype. Hosted at Texas A&M University, the event invited 120 schools to put forward their innovative ideas and designs to the test. Each team's plan was judged by a panel of highly qualified engineers and employees from SpaceX and Tesla.

My Role

My primary role on the team was to design and render the team's proposed Hyperloop pod. A part of my job was to ensure that all the parts and subsystems had sufficient space. Furthermore, I had to ensure that none of the moving components would interfere with each other and that the necessary safety measures were in place. The most difficult challenge was to add all the features the team wanted within the dimensions specified by the organizers while ensuring there was sufficient space for the passengers to be comfortably seated. Part of my job involved analyzing components to determine how they would perform with various amounts of stress and strain. This was done using the stress analysis tools provided in Autodesk Inventor.

Knowledge, Skills and Abilities

  • Computer Aided Design
  • Stress Analysis
  • Virtual wind tunnel analysis
  • Failure Mode Safety Analysis

Goals, Success Factors

  • Successfully represent McMaster at the first ever competition

Challenges

  • Having our laptops stolen when traveling to the competition

Reflection

It was amazing being a part of such a forward-thinking group. The energy was almost electric when we worked. Everyone was passionate about all the new technologies coming out of SpaceX and Tesla. We all jumped at the opportunity to learn a lot more while being a part of such a futuristic competition. Working with our project supervisors, we learned a lot about the mass transportation industry and all the things that needed to be considered when designing a rail system. We faced a lot of challenges at the competition as our car was broken into and electronics were stolen. We frantically attempted to piece together the presentation minutes before our deadline and managed to present. Ever since, I have always remembered to back up my documents online as well as keep multiple copies on USBs as backup. A lot of lessons about protecting our data were learned the hard way in this project, but it was still a great experience overall.
