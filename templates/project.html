McMaster EcoCar 3

2013-2014

McMaster University

Project Description

EcoCAR 3 is the latest in the U.S. Department of Energy (DOE) Advanced Vehicle Technology Competition (AVTC) series. AVTC represents a unique collaboration between government, industry, and academic partners who combine resources to explore sustainable transportation solutions. Sixteen North American Universities have been challenged to re-engineer a Chevrolet Camaro into a hybrid-electric vehicle over a course of 4 years.

My Role

This project involved many different roles, as each team was required to design, simulate, modify, and test the vehicle powertrains. During the year I was involved, I worked on the low-level software development of the car. I helped program new functionality and, in the process, learned a great deal about the development process of the car. I also applied my knowledge of C# to program Freescale processors for specific tasks. Before writing the code, my team and I were required to create appropriate documentation, including interface specifications and test scenarios to validate the logic before compiling the code. This allowed me to apply my knowledge of software development and create appropriate documentation before implementing efficient code.

Knowledge, Skills and Abilities

  • Low Level Programming
  • C#
  • Matlab/Simulink
  • PCB Debugging

Goals, Success Factors

  • Programming the control board for a custom thermoelectric generator.
  • Design a Human-Machine Interface mount for the car

Challenges

  • This project required a very steep learning curve.

Reflection

As amazing as this project was, I left feeling very underprepared for my role. As a second-year student, I knew very little, so I spent most of my time learning about CAN and I2C, among other things. This was an amazing learning experience, and the organization of the team greatly impacted how I managed future projects.
